## What was built

1. **Retry inside the panel** (`features/configure-alerts`). `useFailedWrites()` reads the mutation cache: per rule, the latest write, if it failed. A failed edit, switch or delete shows in its row ("Not saved. <reason>" / "Not deleted. …") with Retry and Dismiss, after the switch in tab order; a failed new rule shows above the list ("“Name” wasn't saved. …"). After Retry or Dismiss focus goes to the row (an edit) or to the named list, or the empty-list text (a delete, a new rule: their row goes or gets a new id); a row gone by then falls back to the list. Not a live region: the toast already announced it.
   - Found while testing in the browser: a busy switch was `disabled`, so focus fell to the page's start on every toggle with the keyboard. It is `aria-disabled` now and ignores presses until the save settles.
2. **Anomaly ⚠ in the markets table** is a `Popover` like the alert bell next to it: a tap shows the note.
3. **Offline overlay** remembers where focus was in the app (a `focus` listener on the wrapper) and gives it back when the network returns.
4. **Heat-map cells** are named "+12% (+11.7%)" plus the anomaly; the headers ("Canada", "Dec 24", which says "December 2024" to a screen reader) are read as focus moves. Tests find a cell by its headers.
5. **Map** (`MarketMap.tsx`): the keyboard focus ring is a separate ring with a card-coloured halo (`FocusRing`), drawn last, over neighbours and patterns; Tab to a market whose middle is outside a zoomed view moves the map to it at once (same zoom, or the zoom a cut-short flight was heading to), and its tooltip waits until the country is in view. All of it on keyboard focus only (`:focus-visible`): a tap focuses after its pointerup.

## Tests

It fails without the map change (checked).

## Independent review (2026-11-05)

No critical findings; lint, types, unit and E2E green. Fixed on the branch:

- **Important:** a failure could be retried twice (row and toast), a failed create then left a ghost "Saving…" row and a 409: both buttons now go through one check (still the rule's latest write, still failed), and the row's Retry and Dismiss close the toast (one toast id per rule).
- **Important:** Dismiss brought back an older failure of the same rule, with a stale Retry: it now forgets every settled write for the rule.
- **Important:** on a touch screen a tap drew the keyboard ring and could move the map (a regression of this branch): gated on `:focus-visible`; an E2E tap case fails on the old code.
- Minor: focus after Retry/Dismiss went to an unnamed container without a ring; a focus request whose row was gone stayed pending; the offline overlay sent focus back after the user had clicked away, and could scroll; "Mar 26" could read as 26 March; big countries were re-centred on every Tab; the tooltip was placed after a fixed two frames; the ring re-rendered every market; unnamed popovers; CSS locators in tests.
- Deferred to the backlog: the alerts sheet stays usable behind the offline notice; a failure in the panel is forgotten after ~5 minutes; "Not saved. " with an empty reason.

The reviewer could not confirm the CI E2E step had run: it had, on the PR into `develop` for 0.10.0 (5 min 58 s in all, E2E with the build 1 min 26 s), so that backlog item stays deleted.
