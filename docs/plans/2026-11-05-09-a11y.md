## What was built

1. **Retry inside the panel** (`features/configure-alerts`). `useFailedWrites()` reads the mutation cache: per rule, the latest write, if it failed. A failed edit, switch or delete shows in its row ("Not saved. <reason>" / "Not deleted. …") with Retry and Dismiss, after the switch in tab order; a failed new rule shows above the list ("“Name” wasn't saved. …"). After Retry or Dismiss focus goes to the row (an edit) or to the list (a delete, a new rule: their row goes or gets a new id). Not a live region: the toast already announced it.
   - Found while testing in the browser: a busy switch was `disabled`, so focus fell to the page's start on every toggle with the keyboard. It is `aria-disabled` now and ignores presses until the save settles.
2. **Anomaly ⚠ in the markets table** is a `Popover` like the alert bell next to it: a tap shows the note.
3. **Offline overlay** remembers where focus was in the app (a `focus` listener on the wrapper) and gives it back when the network returns.
4. **Heat-map cells** are named "+12% (+11.7%)" plus the anomaly; the headers ("Canada", "Dec 24") are read by the screen reader as focus moves. Tests find a cell by its headers.
5. **Map** (`MarketMap.tsx`): the keyboard focus ring is a separate ring with a card-coloured halo, drawn last, over neighbours and patterns; Tab to a market outside a zoomed view moves the map to it at once (same zoom), and its tooltip follows two frames later.

## Tests

- Unit: inline Retry by keyboard (toggle), a failed new rule (Dismiss, then Retry), a failed delete; the busy switch keeps focus; the ⚠ popover opens on a press; focus back after offline; heat-map names without the market.
- E2E: Retry in the row by keyboard inside the modal sheet; offline keeps the focused control; heat-map focus test finds cells by headers; new `e2e/map.spec.ts` (desktop): zoom in, focus Japan off the edge, it comes into view, its tooltip inside the map, the ring is the last path. It fails without the map change (checked).
